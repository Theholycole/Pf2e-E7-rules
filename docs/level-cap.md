# Where to put the level cap

The standard party (fighter, rogue, wizard, cleric) at each level in the combat simulator, 100 fights each against on-level foes: a moderate boss 2 levels up, and four foes 2 levels down. **Damage taken** is the danger score (share of the party’s HP lost, +0.25 a PC downed, more for deaths and wipes): lower means the party is stronger for its level. **Classes above average** counts how many of the 25 rated classes (docs/class-power.md in the simulator) are stronger at that level than their own 1–20 average.

Spikes land on the odd levels where proficiency increases and new class features arrive (3, 5, 7, 13, 17). The worst stretch is 8th–10th, where foes gain on the party before the 11th-level proficiency bumps. Stopping at 5 or 7 caps the game right as the party feels strongest; 8–10 would cap it in a slump.
